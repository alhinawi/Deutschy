- IDS grammis systematic grammar: https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/systematische-grammatik/2112 - alternatives that leave the main assertion valid; egal ob, W + auch immer, sei ... auch noch so, and clause positioning.
- IDS grammis systematic grammar: https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/systematische-grammatik/2109 - concession and the different scope of concessive constructions. No claim that all concessive markers are interchangeable.
- IDS grammis systematic grammar: https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/systematische-grammatik/2121 - restricted validity, knowledge limits, insofern ... als, was ... betrifft.
- IDS connector dictionary: https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/konnektoren/407127 - es sei denn exceptions; main-clause and dass-clause realizations; exception not asserted as fact.
- IDS grammis syntactic classification: https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/systematische-grammatik/2565 - noch in the initial field when linking clauses with weder, und as a contrasting pattern, aber placement.
- IDS terminology: https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/terminologie/87 - Futurperfekt formation, future completion and inference about an earlier event.
- IDS school grammar: https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/sgt/2229 - Futur II formation and modal readings.

All seven pages were retrieved and read on the date above. This is a linguistic-reference task: general reviews/community opinion are not evidence for the grammar rules. The source base is one research institution's systematic grammar, dictionary, terminology and school reference, not independent corroboration across institutions. Source exercises were not copied.
