# Word formation extension

Existing separable verbs and grammar were checked first. Four focused lessons
add compound heads, nominal suffix examples, separable/inseparable contrast and
limits of prefix guessing. Two self-check questions each. No claim that prefixes
always preserve one meaning or that arbitrary suffixes make valid new words.
