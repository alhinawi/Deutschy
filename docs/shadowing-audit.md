# Reading aloud and shadowing

Original existing reading texts across A1-C2 are split at punctuation into short
practice segments. Unit check reconstructs every original text exactly except
whitespace, preventing dropped words or punctuation. These are punctuation-based
segments, not a claim of perfect linguistic sentence segmentation.

Explicit playback only, German-language browser voices only, selectable speed,
stop, next/previous, optional hidden text. A text/voice/rate change cancels audio.
Stale audio callbacks cannot overwrite current status. No microphone, recording,
automated accent rating or fabricated mastery. Speech may need the internet
according to the device's voice provider. Without German voices the text and
manual practice remain available; playback stays disabled.

All six level selections checked at 390px with no horizontal overflow and no
WCAG A/AA axe findings. Browser mock checks German-only voice filtering and
selection, language, completion prompt, playback error, navigation cancellation
and text hiding. Mock results do not verify actual device speech quality.
Mobile no-voice screenshot inspected. Unit/typecheck/build/export pass.
