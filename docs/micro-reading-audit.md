# Original micro-readings

Eight new original fictional texts, two per A1-B2 study grouping. Each is under
90 words and has three distinct comprehension questions. A1 short everyday
clauses; A2 past events/reasons; B1 collaboration and concrete feedback; B2
qualified comparison and interpreting evidence. Groupings are editorial practice,
not official exam placement. No current prices, policies or measured statistics.

Uses the existing reader, answer reveal, translation and local practice points.
Hub explicitly says points are not exam grades. New texts also enter existing
search, dictation and shadowing through the reading pipeline. No duplicate
reader or invented progress. Original eight route and question-answer flows
checked: 24 reveals, result screens, no mobile overflow and no WCAG A/AA axe
findings. Unit/typecheck/build/export pass. Native proofreading unverified.
