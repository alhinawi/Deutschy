- Articles: ein/eine forms scoped to nominative; indefinite article has no plural, rather than claiming all plurals lack articles. - Negation: nicht is not invariably the final word. Included a final infinitive/participle and possessive-noun example.
- Imperative: e to i/ie persists but a to ä does not (Fahr!, not Fähr!); Warte! shows needed -e. Acknowledges inclusive wir in addition to the three direct-address forms.
