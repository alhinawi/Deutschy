## Evidence and limits

Per-record extracted fields and actual lookup URLs are in `tenth-noun-audit.json`. Source responses were fetched through the German Wiktionary revision API on the review date, with a backoff after rate limiting. A parser match is a consistency check, not native-editor certification or an official CEFR vocabulary claim. Multiple meanings and regional forms can exist; this pass confirms the selected article/plural is accepted, not that it is the only accepted form. Compounds and proper names are explicitly marked as manual morphology reviews rather than fabricated dictionary hits.
