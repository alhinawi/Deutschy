# C1 grammar source ledger - 2026-11-16

This is a focused advanced practice selection, not a claim of an official C1 curriculum. German and Egyptian Arabic explanations and all exercises are original and have not had native proofreading. Sources verify the grammatical distinctions, not the quality or CEFR level of every authored sentence. No source exercise is copied.

## Sources read

- IDS grammis, Zifonun/Strecker, edited by Donalies: https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/systematische-grammatik/1001 - both Konjunktiv I and II in unreal comparisons.
- IDS grammis connector reference: https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/konnektoren/407056 - finite verb follows standalone comparative als; distinguish temporal als.
- IDS grammis, Zifonun/Strecker: https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/systematische-grammatik/999 - zu ... als dass, unreal consequences and ohne dass; Konjunktiv not the only possible mood in all such contexts.
- IDS grammis, Strecker: https://grammis.ids-mannheim.de/systematische-grammatik/232 - perfect infinitive, haben/sein and Partizip II; zu placement in the form table.
- Deutsche Grammatik 2.0, specialist teaching reference: https://deutschegrammatik20.de/spezielle-verben/modalverben/subjektive-modalverben-vermutung/ - strong inference, probability, possibility and exclusion. No invented fixed percentage scale.
- Deutsche Grammatik 2.0: https://deutschegrammatik20.de/spezielle-verben/modalverben/subjektive-modalverben-distanzierung/ - sollen reports another source; wollen reports the subject's own claim. Neither construction alone proves truth or falsehood.
- Deutsche Grammatik 2.0: https://deutschegrammatik20.de/spezielle-verben/modalverben/subjektive-modalverben-zeitformen/ - earlier event uses perfect infinitive; passive uses worden sein.

These are two source types: linguistic reference and specialist language instruction. General reviews or community opinion were not appropriate evidence for these grammar rules. All were fetched and read on the date above. Source links are also available per topic in the practice page.

## Check criteria

Five sets of eight questions. Each row has one intended correct option; prompts specify Konjunktiv I/II where both are otherwise possible. Meaning questions supply the intended subjective/reporting context. Inflection drills avoid presenting legitimate alternative moods as universally wrong. Automated shape and browser-answer checks supplement, but do not replace, expert proofreading.
