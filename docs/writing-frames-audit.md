# Practical writing frames

2026-11-16: eight frames, two per A1/A2/B1/B2 grouping, linked directly to existing original writing tasks/models. Covers informal introduction/invitation, formal appointment/problem report, application/group proposal, complaint/editor letter. This is task-style practice, not an official exam format, marking scheme or promise. No original exam tasks or brand-specific score/time rules copied.

General task structure reference checked: https://bfu.goethe.de/a1_sd1/schreiben.php and https://bfu.goethe.de/b1_mod/schreiben.php support attending to task points, greeting, organization and closing. We do not claim these eight models meet every exam provider's rules. Learners are told to follow their actual task.

Frames use visible slots with a warning that endings/prepositions may need adjustment. Models are the same objects used in the existing trainer, labeled one possible fictional answer. Checklist is manually ticked, never an automatic language grade. Drafts stay in tab memory, preserve edits across task switches and are not sent/persisted to accounts. No legal remedy or actual availability claim from fictional complaint/appointment details.

German/Arabic internal review: formal Sie/Ihre, polite requests, subordinate clause endings, greeting/closing, each frame against its source brief. New noun phrases include einen Termin, die Stelle, Ihren Beitrag; existing vocabulary article/plural records unchanged. No native human proofread.
