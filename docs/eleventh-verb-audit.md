# Verb tables review - 2026-11-16

143 verb records, 858 displayed present forms, 143 third-person preterite forms, 143 participles and 143 selected auxiliaries reviewed. 23 separable records and 12 reflexive records included. Zero morphology spelling corrections justified; do not invent a fix count.

## Method

Fetched current German Wiktionary overview wikitext with revision IDs for 142 unique base lemmas. Singular present, preterite, participle and auxiliary compared to the source fields. The ihr form was cross-checked against the identical imperative-plural form except six modals (full Lingolia modal table). Wir/sie forms checked through the infinitive rule, separable-prefix placement and sein exception. Reflexive pronouns stripped only for base-form comparison; all six pronoun positions checked against the separate reflexive source. These are explicit rule-based checks, not 858 independent dictionary table rows. All selected auxiliaries are attested, not asserted exclusive to every meaning or region.

Expanded conjugation pages were fetched for 16 verbs before rate limiting. The 429 was respected; no claim of 143 expanded pages. Source overview plus the cited grammar rules cover the remaining checks.

The separate trainer has 4 verbs x 11 forms x 6 persons = 264 cells. All base arrays and composed forms reviewed against current full Verbformen tables (sein/haben/machen/kommen), including Konjunktiv past, Futur II and würde forms. Existing accepted short variants remain. No trainer morphology correction needed.

## Useful fixes

The modal pages' old bare Perfekt table could be overgeneralized to a sentence with another infinitive. Added an explicit limitation and original contextual examples with Ersatzinfinitiv. Added the corresponding lassen contrast with a variant caveat, a selected-auxiliary limitation and per-verb dictionary links. No previously accepted form was deleted. Snapshot regressions retain all checked values.
