# Noun photo relevance screen 173

2026-11-18. All 965 noun thumbnails were screened in 25 contact sheets. Eight uncertain originals were enlarged: Käfer, Pflaster, Adventskranz, Schraubenmutter, Heftklammer, Essstäbchen, Sieb and Spielplatz. All original files remained unchanged between the 170 contact-sheet preparation and main at 172. Inventory IDs and ordering were checked against current vocabulary before this batch.

This is a relevance/suitability screen, not a native-language sign-off or a fresh license audit of all 965 older photographs. Relationship and occupation photos remain contextual examples with existing notices. No noun meanings, articles, plurals, IDs, exercises, order or progress storage changed.

## Review ledger and credit links

`data/noun-photo-audit173.json` records all 965 IDs, contact-sheet position, review scope/date, decision, before/after SHA-256 and the file/source/license/author/padding metadata. The guard checks all reviewed files and credit fields, but does not pretend a hash establishes visual correctness. A future change should be visually checked before deliberately updating its ledger record. Future new words remain separate editorial work.

`photoLicenseUrl` replaces the nested credit-link conditions with exact supported CC BY/BY-SA versions 2.0, 2.5, 3.0 and 4.0, plus CC0. Unknown, localized or mixed labels remain plain text rather than being mapped to a potentially wrong license. Regression tests exercise all supported versions and malformed/unsupported inputs. Existing source, model, padding and crop notices remain.
