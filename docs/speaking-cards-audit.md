# Speaking cards A1-B2

12 original prompt/model/follow-up sets, three per practice grouping. Models are
explicitly fictional training examples, not user biography or accounts of real
people. A1 uses short everyday turns, A2 adds past events and polite clarification,
B1 adds planning/reasons/problem descriptions, B2 adds balanced arguments,
objections and conditional conclusions. These are editorial study groupings,
not official exam prompts or certified placement.

Models are hidden until requested and reset on task changes. Per-task keywords
and self-checks remain temporarily in the tab, not stored, sent or scored.
Optional model playback uses the German-only browser voice component, with
no-voice and playback-error fallbacks. No microphone on this page.

All 12 revealed models tested at 390px with no overflow and no WCAG A/AA axe
findings. Notes/checks retained when switching tasks; model reset verified.
Unit/typecheck/build/export pass. Mobile screenshot inspected. No independent
native-speaker proofreading or real device voice-quality check.
